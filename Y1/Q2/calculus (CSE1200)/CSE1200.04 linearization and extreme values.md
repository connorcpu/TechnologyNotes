- definitie van een dirivative: $f'(a) = \displaystyle\lim_{h\to 0} {{f(a+h)-f(a)}\over{h}}$ if this limit exists
- tangent line is raaklijn, als je het punt weet en de richtingscoefficient weet, yada yada dit is simpel
   - $y = m(x-a)+b$ gaat door punt (a, b)
   - $y = f(a) + f'(a)(x-a)$
   - $z = f_x(a,b)(x-a) + f_y(y-b) + f(a,b)$
   - second dirivative f''(x)
   - 3e: $f^{(3)}(x)$
   - 4e: $\frac {df}{dx^4}$
- regels:
   - sum: $(f + g)' = f' + g'$
   - product: $fg' + f'g$
   - quotient (don't use): ${{fg' + f'g}\over{g^2}}$
   - chain (subsitute quotient with this): $(f(g(x)))' = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$
- standart derivatives:
   - $\frac d {dx}x^r = r \cdot x^{r-1}$ (standart power rule polynomial shit)
   - $\frac d {dx}ln(x) = \frac 1 x$
   - $\frac d {dx} \sin(x) = \cos(x)$
   - $\frac d {dx}\cos(x) = -\sin(x)$
   - $\frac d {dx}e^x = e^x$
   - $\frac d {dx} a^x = ln(a) \cdot a^x$
