- $\displaystyle\int_1^\infin {x \over 1 + x^2}dx = \lim_{b\to\infin}[\frac 1 2\ln(1+x^2)]_1^b$
- $\displaystyle\lim_{b\to\infin} \frac 1 2 \ln(1+b^2)-{\ln(2) \over 2}$
- answer is divergent
