## 1
$$\frac 1 {5-x}$$
- question: which option is a power series representation of the previous expression
- answer: $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty (\frac x 5)^n$ (zolang |x|<1) (all answers are correct)
- because: $\frac 1 {1-x} = \sum x^n$

## 2
$$\frac 1 5 \sum_{n=0}^\infty (\frac x 5)^n$$
- question: when is the above a valid representation of $\frac 1 {5-x}$
- answer: -5 < x < 5
- because:  |x/5| < 1

## 3
$$\frac 1 2 \sum_{n=0}^\infty (\frac {x-3} 2)^n$$
- question: when is the above a valid representation of $\frac 1 {5-x}$
- answer: 1 < x < 5
- because: omschrijven van fractie naar summation; again, to $\frac 1 {1-x}$ where x is some goat and |x| < 1
