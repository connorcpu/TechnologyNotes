# sequences
- a sequence is a function where $f: \N \to \R$
- they behave just like functions but beware, sometime the interval behaviour matters
- notation: usually $(a_n)$ or $\displaystyle (a_n)_{n=k}^\infiy$ (latter one means from k to infinity)
- convergent divergent applies
- definition of a limit is the same

# excerizes

## 1
consider the following sequence: $a_0 = 3$; $a_{n+1} = - \frac 1 3 a_n$
which equation holds for all $n \in \N$?
answer: $a_n = {(-1)^n \over 3^{n-1}}$ you can tell by the base cases.
