### the squeeze theorem
- $f(x) \leq g(x) \leq h(x)$ when x is near but not at a
- $\displaystyle \lim_{x\to a} f(x) = L = \lim_{x\to a} h(x)$
- $\therefore \displaystyle \lim_{x\to a} g(x) = L$
- $\because$ g(x) tussen f(x) en h(x) maar das t zelfde dus is gelijk aan alles    
example: $f(x) = x^2\ sin (\frac{1}{x})$, wat is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0}f(x)$
- $-1 \leq \sin(\frac{1}{x}) \leq 1$
- $\therefore -x^2 \leq x^2 \sin(\frac{1}{x}) \leq x^2$
- $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0} -x^2 = 0 \land \lim_{x\to 0} x^2 = 0$
- $\therefore \displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0}f(x) =0$
