## geometric series 
take the original series expressed as $a_i$, if $\exist r ({a_{i + 1} \over a_i} = r)$  
example: $\frac 1 2$, $\frac 1 4$, $\frac 1 8$... ${\frac 1 4 \over \frac 1 2} = \frac 1 2$ so $r = \frac 1 2$  
a geometric series can be written as $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^\infty c \cdot r^n$   
uhm, limiet naar oneindige gesomt?: 

$$ \begin{align}
   \displaystyle
   &\sum_{j=0}^\infty cn^j&= \\
      c + n &\sum_{j=1}^\infty cn^{j-1} &= \\
      k &= j - 1 \\
      c + n&\sum_{k=0}^\infty cn^k &=
   \end{align}$$  
   
where $k=j-1$
$\displaystyle(1-n)\sum_{j=0}^\infty cn^j = c \iff \sum_{j=0}^\infty cn^j = {c \over 1-n}$  
als $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n$ convergeert, dan $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty} a_n = 0$; this can be used to prove a series is _not_ convergent
dat limiet naar 0 gaat betekend niet per se dat reeks convergent is
