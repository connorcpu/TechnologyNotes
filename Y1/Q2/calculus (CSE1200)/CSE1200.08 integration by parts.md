## integration by parts 
integration counterpart to the product rule:
1. $\frac d {dx} (f(x)g(x)) = f(x)g'(x) + f'(x)g(x)$
2. $\int f(x)g'(x) + f'(x)g(x) dx = f(x)g(x)$
3.  $\int f(x)g'(x) dx + \int f'(x)g(x) dx = f(x)g(x)$
- $\because \int f(x)+g(x) dx = \int f(x) dx + \int g(x) dx$
4. $\int f(x)g'(x) dx = f(x)g(x) - \int f'(x)g(x) dx$  
or  
$\int_a^b f(x)g'(x) dx = [f(x)g(x)]_a^b-\int_a^bf'(x)g(x)dx$ for definite integrals  

## LIATE
when applying integration by parts you should choose f(x) in order of LIATE
- **L**ogarithms
- **I**nverse trigonometric functions
- **A**lgebraic functions 
- **T**rigonometric functions
- **E**xponentials
