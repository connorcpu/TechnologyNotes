## rekenregels
- $\displaystyle\int^b_a c \space dx = c(b-a)$
- $\displaystyle\int^b_a [f(x)+g(x)] dx =\int^b_af(x)dx+\int^b_ag(x)dx$
- $\displaystyle\int^b_a c\cdot f(x)dx = c\int^b_a f(x) dx$
- $\displaystyle\int^b_af(x)dx+\int^c_bf(x)dx = \int_a^cf(x)dx$
- $\displaystyle\int_a^bf(x)dx = -\int_b^af(x)dx$
