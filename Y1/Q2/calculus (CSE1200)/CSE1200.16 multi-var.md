# recap on vectors
- op vector $(x_1, y_1) \land (x_2, y_2)$
- size & direction
- n-dimentional, n-tuple
- really not wierd to have 3+ dimentions
- tippically used bracket (square) matrix-notation $\begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix}$; alternativly wierd flattended < and > $\lang and \rang$
- since only size & direction, starting location does not matter in the slightest
- optellen, $x_1 + x_2; y_1 + y_2$
- aftrekken: b - a zorgt ervoor dat vector van einde van a naar einde van b loopt
- schalen: $x_1 * c; y_1 * c$
- dot-product: 
   - werkt alleen op zelfde dimentionale vector
   - scalair van 2 vectoren punts-gewijs te vermenigvuldigen, en dan op te tellen
   - $x_1 x_2 + y_1 y_2$
   - dot-product is distrubitief
- angles:
   - let a & b be vectors in $\R^n$
   - $a \cdot b = |a| |b| \cos(\theta)$
   - ${a \cdot b \over |a| |b|} = \cos(\theta)$
   - direct result of cosine rule: $c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \cos(\theta)$
   - $|b-a|^2 = |a|^2 + |b|^2 - 2|a||b|\cos(\theta)$
   - $|b|^2 - 2a\cdot b + |a|^2 = |a|^2 + |b|^2 - 2|a||b|\cos(\theta)$
   - altijd tussen 0 en $\pi$ want is de kleine hoek
   - $a \cdot b = 0$ means a is orthogonal to b (90 degrees?)
