- cumulative distribution function (verdelings functie) is de kans dat de waarde onder een bepaalde waarde zit.
   - since the chance has to increase for a higher bound you must have a increasing function
   - $f(y) = \int_{-\infty}^y$
- chance of random var being between a and b is the integral of the chance function from a to b
- probablility density function is the normal probablility curve
- distribution function is the integral of the density function
- frequentie histogram is de bar chart van de kans dat de waarde tussen de integrers zit
- median
   - the point on the CDF (the increasing one) where the output is 0.5
   - medians are the average of the middelse waardes
- quantiles
   - just solve the integral for a given value
   - "first quartile" is where the CDF is 0.25
- bernouli distribution
   - chance of success or failure, no other options
- binomial distrubution
   - B_{in} = \frac n k \cdot p^k \cdot (1-p)^{n-k}$
- geometric distribution 
   - ga door totdat success
   - $(1-p)^k+1 \cdot p$
   - geheugenloos, je kan van de hoeveelheid falen niet gebruiken om de volgende kans te berekenen
- exponentieel
   - $f(x) = \lambda e^{-\lambda x}$
- normaal
   - $f(x) = {1 \over \sqrt{2\pi \sigma}}e^{- {(x-\mu)^2 \over 2 \sigma ^2}}$
