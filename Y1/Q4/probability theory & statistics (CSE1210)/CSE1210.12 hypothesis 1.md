- hypothesis testing
   - null hypothesis is what the hypothesis is in the case nothing special is happening
   - the alternative hypothesis is the hypothesis that states something special is happening
   - 0.95 is the standard value considered beyond reasonable doubt

- test statistic and p-values
   - p-value is the chance that assuming $H_0$ is true the observed values occur
   - p-value is _not_ the chance that $H_0$ is true
- asymptotic test for a proportion
- t-test for the mean
