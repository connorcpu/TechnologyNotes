- recurrance equations
- of the form $\begin{cases} T_0 = C_0 & n > 0 \\ T_n = 2T(n-1) + C_1\end{cases}$
- so fist we need to find the equation for t_n based not on n-1 but n-k:
$$
\begin{align}
t(n) =& 2T(n-1) + C_1 \\
t(n-1) =& 2T(n-2) + C_1 \\
t(n) =& 2(2T(n-2) + C_1) + C_1 \\
t(n) =& 4T(n-2) + 3C_1 \\
t(n) =& 2^kT(n-k) + (2^k-1)C_1 \\
\end{align}
$$
$$
\begin{rcases}
\begin{rcases}
k = n \\
t(n) = 2^kT(n-k) + (2^k-1)C_1
\end{rcases}
&t(n) = 2^nT(n-n) + (2^n - 1)C_1 \\
&t(0) = C_0
\end{rcases}
t(n) = 2^nC_0 + (2^n-1)C_1
$$
