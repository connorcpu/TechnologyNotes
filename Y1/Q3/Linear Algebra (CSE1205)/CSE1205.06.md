- determinants
   - $\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} = ad-bc$
   - if you linearly transform a shape, the area of the shape is multiplied with the determinant
   - singular matrix means not inversable ($\det a = 0$)
- invertability
   - $A\cdot A^{-1} = I$
   - $A\vec x = b \to bA^{-1} = \vec x$
   - for a 2x2 matrix: $\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d\end{bmatrix}^{-1} = {1\over ad-bc}\begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a\end{bmatrix}$
   - it's possible to just wang a matrix instead of a vector into an augmented matrix, then use row reduction to turn [A|I] into [I|A]
