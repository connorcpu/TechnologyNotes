# endterm stuff
- algebraic multiplicity is how many times one solution occurs in the characteristic polynomial ($\lambda = 2 \lor \lambda = -4 \lor \lambda = 2) gives algebraic multiplicity 1 for -4 and 2 for 2
- geometric multiplicity is the dimesion of the eigenspace, it's the amount of free variables when solving $(A-\lambda I)x = 0$
- $1 \leq geometric multiplicity \leq algebraic multiplicity$
- to find the basis eigenspace of a diagonalisable matrix $A = PDP^{-1}$ find the basis of the eigenspace of D (eigenvalues are the same for D and A) then multiply each vector by P
- rank of a column is the dimention of the column space, aka the amount of piviots you have in the echelon form
- to do the power of a matrix, diagonalize it and then do the power of the diagonal matrix BY MULTIPLYING THE DIAGONAL WITH THE POWER **DO NOT POWER THE ENTRIES**
- similar matrices have the same eigenvalues but the eigenvectors are  multiplied with matrix P in A = PDP^{-1}
- to make a diagonal matrix, get the eigenvalues and the eigenvectors, then put the eigenvalues on the diagonal of D and the eigenvectors in P in the same column as it's eigenvalue
