- cofactor expansion
   - determinant van een NxN matrix is based on the (n-1)x(n-1) matrix.
   - cofactor of a given entry in the matrix is $(-1)^{i+j} \det(a_{ij})$ where $a_{ij}$ is the submatrix you get when you cross out row i and column j
   - sum the cofactors of the first row to find the determinant
   - if the sum of the coordinates is uneven multiply by -1 
      - (matrix gets checkerboard pattern, top left is positive)
      - this is what $(-1)^{i+j}$ does
   - take the first entry of the first row, get the determinant of the 2x2 matrix you get if you remove the row and colunm of the entry.
   - multiply that my the entry's value, then by the -1 raised to the sum of the coordinatates.
   - then do this for every entry in the row, sum all of the rest.
   - this can be done for every row.
   - this can be done for every column.
   - example: $\begin{bmatrix}0 & 3 & 0 \\ 5 & 6 & 7 \\ 2 & -4 & 0\end{bmatrix}$
      - cofactor expantion of the second row:
      - cofactor of the 5: $(-1)^{i+j} = -1$; $a_{ij} = \begin{bmatrix}3 & 0 \\ -4 & 0\end{bmatrix}$
      - cofactor of the 5: $-1 \cdot (3\cdot 0) - (-4\cdot 0)) = -1 \cdot (0-0) = 0$
      - cofactor of the 6: $(-1)^{2+2} \cdot \det(\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 2 & 0\end{bmatrix})$
      - cofactor of the 6: $1 \cdot (0 - 0) = 0$
      - cofactor of the 7: $(-1)^{2+3}\cdot \det(\begin{bmatrix}0&3 \\2&-4\end{bmatrix})$
      - cofactor of the 7: $-1 \cdot (0 - 6) = 6$
      - weigh the cofactors: 5 * 0; 6 * 0; 7 * 6
      - sum: 0+0+42 = 42

- determinants and elementary row operations
   - row exchange B to get A gives det B = -det A
   - multiply one row of B with k to get A gives det B = k det A
   - add a multiple of a row of B to a row of A gives det B = det A
- determinants and invertibility
   - $\det A = \det A^t$
- $\det AB = \det A \det B$
- $\det A^{-1} = {1 \over \det A}$
