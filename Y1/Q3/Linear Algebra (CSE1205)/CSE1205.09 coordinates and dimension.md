- if you have a basis of a subspace, every point in the subspace is a linear combination of the vectors in the basis
   - this means you can index any point in the subspace as a multiple of the basis vectors
   - the basis vectors act just like a matrix because ofcourse
   - notation:
      - $\B$ is the basis of the subspace, as a set of vectors
      - $[x]_\B$ is the coordinates of the vector x by the basis of the subspace, it can be multiplied with $\B$ as if it was a matrix to produce $x$
- the dimention of a non-zero subspace W is the number of vectors for any basis
   - notation: $\dim W$
   - $\dim {0} = 0$
   - so a basis of a plane subspace = 2
   - this means that for a p-dimentional subspace, any linear independend p-sized set of vectors is a basis
- the rank of matrix A (notation: $rank A$ is the dimension of the column space
- if matrix A has n columns, the rank of A plus the dimention of the nul space equals n
