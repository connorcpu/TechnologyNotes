   - span of the columns of A (as vectors)

- null space
   - the set of solution for $A\vec x = 0$
- basis of a subspace
   - the minimum set of linear independent vectors that you need to span the subspace

      - reduce to echelon form
      - identify pivot columns
      - basis is pivot columns OF ORIGINAL MATRIX
   - to find basis of null space of matrix A
      - solve $A\vec x = 0$
      - convert to parametric form
      - the vectors that occur are the basis
