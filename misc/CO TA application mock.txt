The change I would make to CO with the new curriculum, is to reduce the amount of arithmetic questions on the exam. I feel that more essay-like questions would be better suited for this subject, since CO is about how a given system works. Being able to convert binary is important. However, I feel, instead of reasoning about how many bits would be needed to encode ALU operations, questions could be more in the direction of "describe why a CISC CPU with a given change would not work or work better than a different solution". Alternatively I would suggest going more in-depth on RISC architecture since that is what the book mostly focuses on, or exchanging the abstract discussion about 'some' hypothetical architecture, for a specific example, such as x86 or ARM.
