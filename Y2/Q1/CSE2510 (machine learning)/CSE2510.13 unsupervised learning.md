- k-means
   - "partition data into k-clusters" when programmer picks k.
   - centroid is the mean of the cluster
   - "minimize the total euclidean distance of all points to their assigned cluster centres"
   - 1. put k centroids on
   - 2. assign closest centroid to data point for each point
   - 3. then recomputer centroid 
   - 4. repeat step 2 & 3, until critereon is met
   - critereon could be:
      - no more (or less than a number) re-assignments of data points
		- no or small change in centroids
